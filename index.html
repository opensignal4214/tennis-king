COURT KING

semi-3D tennis
Difficulty
Play
On-screen controls
Turn off if you're using a connected controller
Left hand WASD: move, and aim by holding a direction as you swing — A/D pull the ball left/right, W goes deep, S plays it short.
Right hand strokes (home row): J = top spin · K = slice · L = flat · ; = drop shot · I = lob.
At the net the same keys volley; against a high ball they hit an overhead. Your position, the incoming ball, and your timing decide the shot.
Timing: a ring closes on the incoming ball — swing when it's green. Mistimed swings go weak, wide, or long.
Serve: WASD places the target anywhere in the box, then press J / K / L to toss — press the same key again at the top of the toss to strike: J = top spin (slower, safe, bounces high) · K = slice (curves wide, stays low) · L = flat (fastest).
Touch: left pad = move & aim (hold a direction as you swing). Right pad = swing: press · flick toward a shot (↑ lob, ↖ topspin, ← slice, ↙ flat, ↓ drop), no flick = topspin · hold to charge · lift to hit. Serve: tap the right pad to toss, tap again to strike (tap high = kick, middle = slice, low = flat); aim by dragging the left pad.
